---
title: "On beginning"
date: 2026-05-24
---

Every beginning carries the ghost of all the beginnings before it. I started this site the same way I start most things — by not starting, by circling, by writing the first line seventeen times.

Then just once more.

The trick, I think, is to lower the stakes enough that you can actually move. This is not a magazine. It is not a statement of intent. It is a place to put things I don't want to lose.

That's all. That's plenty.
