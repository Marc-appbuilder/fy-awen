There is a twenty-minute window in the evening when the light stops being useful and starts being something else entirely.

It goes sideways. It picks out things the midday sun flattened — the texture of a field, the depth of a hedge, the way a hill has several different colours depending on where you're standing.

I try to be outside during this window whenever I can. I don't always manage it. When I do, I feel like I've got away with something.

This photograph doesn't quite capture it. No photograph does. But it points in the right direction.
