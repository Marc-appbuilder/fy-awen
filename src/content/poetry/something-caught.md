---
title: "Something Caught"
date: 2026-05-18
description: "On the small arrests — the light on a wall, a word overheard, the particular quality of a Tuesday afternoon."
---

A word on a wall,
someone else's handwriting,
and suddenly I am eight years old
standing in a different street
in different shoes.

---

The light does this, too.
Arrives at an angle
that the afternoon
had no right to.

I put down what I was carrying
and stood there.

---

You cannot explain
the small arrests —
only note them,
carry them lightly,
let them do their work
in the dark.
