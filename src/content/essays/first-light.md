There is a particular quality to early morning light — the way it arrives before you're ready for it, before the world has decided what it's going to be.

I have been thinking about beginnings. Not the clean, declarative kind you write in notebooks or announce at dinner. The quiet ones. The ones that start as a change of light.

## The hour before the hour

I was up at five last Tuesday, not by choice, but the way you sometimes find yourself awake without knowing quite why. The window was grey, then blue, then — for a few minutes — an extraordinary shade of coral that had no business appearing above terraced houses in the rain.

I didn't photograph it. This is the thing I'm learning: some things collapse when you try to hold them. The act of reaching for the phone undoes the moment. You end up with a document of where you were standing instead of the thing that moved you.

> The image is the poor cousin of the experience. It proves the experience happened but cannot give it back.

This is not an argument against photography. It's an argument for being present before you're anything else.

## What inspiration actually feels like

People talk about inspiration as if it arrives fully formed — a bolt, a vision, a voice. In my experience it feels more like a loosening. Something that was held tight begins, tentatively, to move.

You notice a word on a sign and it stays with you for three days. You hear someone laugh in a particular way in a café and find yourself thinking about a person you haven't thought of in years. A song comes on while you're washing up and suddenly you know what you want to write about — not the song, not the memory, but something the two of them opened between them.

This is the only kind of inspiration I trust. The accidental kind.

## A site called Fy Awen

This is my attempt at a record. Not a highlight reel. Not a brand. A record of what moves me, in the hope that putting it somewhere means I don't lose it entirely.

Essays when a thought needs room. Poems when it needs compression. Photos when words won't do it. Notes when I don't quite know what it is yet.

That seems like enough to be going on with.
